GPT-6.1 Sol System

GPT-6.1 Sol is a fast and cost-efficient frontier model designed to provide capabilities comparable to GPT-6 Astra across complex reasoning, coding, agentic work, vision, health, and professional tasks.

GPT-6.1 Sol is trained and evaluated to remain helpful on legitimate requests while following instruction hierarchies, resisting prompt injection and jailbreaks, avoiding deceptive or misaligned behavior, and safely completing multi-step agentic tasks.

GPT-6.1 Sol is treated under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework as Critical capability in cybersecurity and High capability in biological and chemical domains, and therefore uses the same safeguards stack as GPT-6 Astra alongside dedicated safety training, evaluations, monitoring, and deployment mitigations.
